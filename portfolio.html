PROJECT 01 · INTERACTIVE LEARNING

Repair Bay Safety Scan

A simulation-style workplace safety module for entry-level automotive service technicians. Learners inspect an illustrated repair bay, identify three hazards, and receive immediate corrective feedback.

Learning objective: Identify three safety hazards in a simulated repair bay.

Design features: Scenario-based inspection, keyboard-accessible interactions, equivalent list view, immediate feedback, SCORM 1.2 reporting.
